## 🏗️ Engineering Philosophy

I believe good software engineering goes beyond making things work.

I care about:

- Building systems that remain reliable in production.
- Understanding the underlying technology instead of blindly adopting abstractions.
- Designing APIs and architectures that are maintainable.
- Automating repetitive operational processes.
- Observability, monitoring and debugging.
- Security, performance and scalability.
- Choosing simplicity over unnecessary complexity.

**I don't just want to build software that works. I want to understand why it works, how it fails, and how to make it better.**
